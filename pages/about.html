QuickTag LazyMod

TAG FASTER

Tag images faster, right where you browse.

QuickTag adds a small tagging window to the pages where you upload or describe images. Pick tags from your own collections, find them by typing, and let AI suggest tags for the image, all without leaving the page.

It's made for anyone who tags a lot of images: artists, archivists, and people who keep image galleries or booru-style sites organized.

Open the Docs What's new Project website

Why you'll like it

Your tags, one click away

Keep tags in neat collections and click to add or remove them. Start typing to find any tag, even with a typo.

Focus Mode for long sessions

Press Alt+A for a calm full-screen view with the image, your tags, and your collections side by side.

AI that stays on your computer

Get tag suggestions for the image on the page from AI running on your own computer. Your images stay with you.

Ready-made packs, any site

Install tag collections shared by the community in one click, and show QuickTag where each site keeps its tag field.
